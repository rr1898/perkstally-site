# PerksTally support

## Contact

Email [perkstally@gmail.com](mailto:perkstally@gmail.com). Include your phone model and what you were doing when the problem happened.

## Common questions

**A credit amount or reset date is wrong for my card.**
Card benefits change often and can differ by account. Open the perk, tap Edit, and change it to match your card. If the built-in list is wrong for everyone, email us and we'll fix it in the next update.

**I'm not getting reminders.**
Check that notifications for PerksTally are allowed in your phone's settings, and that Expiration reminders is turned on in PerksTally > Settings. Reminders arrive at 9 AM on the reminder day.

**I got a new phone. How do I move my data?**
On the old phone, open Settings > Export backup and save the file somewhere you can reach from the new phone (Files, email, or cloud storage). On the new phone, open Settings > Import backup and pick that file.

**Does PerksTally connect to my bank or see my card number?**
No. PerksTally never asks for card numbers or logins and makes no network requests. Everything you enter stays on your phone.

**How do I delete my data?**
Settings > Erase all data, or uninstall the app.
