# PerksTally Privacy Policy

_Last updated: November 11, 2026_

PerksTally helps you keep track of the credits and benefits that come with your credit cards.

## The short version

PerksTally does not collect, transmit, sell or share any personal data. Everything you enter stays on your device.

## What the app stores

- The cards you choose to add (card product name, an optional nickname, the anniversary date you set, annual fee).
- The perks on those cards and any edits you make to them.
- The usage you log against each perk.
- Your reminder preferences.

This information is saved only in the app's local storage on your device. PerksTally has no accounts and no servers, and the developer cannot see any of it.

## What the app never asks for

PerksTally never asks for and has no field for card numbers, expiry dates, security codes, bank logins or any other payment credentials. It does not connect to your bank or card issuer.

## Notifications

If you turn on reminders, the app schedules notifications on your device. They are created and delivered locally; no push notification service or server is involved.

## Backups

If you choose "Export backup", the app creates a file containing the data listed above and hands it to your device's share sheet. Where that file goes (Files, email, cloud storage) is your choice and is governed by the service you send it to.

## Analytics, advertising and tracking

None. The app contains no analytics, advertising or tracking software.

## Deleting your data

Use Settings > Erase all data, or uninstall the app. Either removes everything PerksTally has stored.

## Children

PerksTally is not directed at children under 13 and does not knowingly collect information from anyone.
